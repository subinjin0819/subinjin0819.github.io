RESEARCH VISION

From conductive biomaterials to adaptive therapeutic interfaces

My next research direction is to move beyond passive electrical conduction and build biointerfaces whose mechanics, chemistry, and electrical function actively adapt to tissue state and regenerative needs.

01

Molecularly programmed biointerfaces

Couple interfacial conductivity with dynamic biochemical and cell-instructive functions.

02

Minimally invasive neural electronics

Replace bulky implants with injectable, conformal, or in-situ-formed neural interfaces.

03

Bioelectronic control of regeneration

Use electrical and material cues together to guide nerve, muscle, and CNS repair.
