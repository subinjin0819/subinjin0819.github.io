    <h2>Research profile</h2>
    <p>My work has centered on hyaluronic-acid-based conductive hydrogels, in-situ-formed electrical interfaces, adhesive regenerative biomaterials, and soft bioelectronic systems. I am particularly interested in translating material properties—injectability, adhesion, conductivity, and dynamic mechanics—into biological and therapeutic function.</p>
